Overview

Abstract

Eco Matrix is a self-regulating, soil-sensing mesh network that keeps city trees and green spaces thriving through heat, drought, and neglect. By combining real-time sensing, autonomous power, and long-range LoRa communication, it provides actionable intelligence to urban foresters and communities.

Why This Matters

Urban trees face extreme heat, compacted soil, erratic watering, and neglect. 62% of young street trees die within the first 5 years due to water stress and poor monitoring. Cities lack real-time data to intervene effectively.

  • No visibility — tree health is invisible until it's too late.
  • Wasted water — timed irrigation wastes up to 40% of water.
  • Manual labour — costly, inefficient inspection rounds.
  • Climate stress — heatwaves and droughts kill trees faster than ever.

Advantages of Eco Matrix

  • Autonomous, off-grid operation — solar-powered, no mains required.
  • Decentralized mesh — no cloud dependency, works offline.
  • Reduces water use by up to 62%.
  • Open protocol — easy integration with city dashboards.

Measurable Outcomes

  • Environmental — cuts water waste, preserves urban canopy, supports biodiversity.
  • Social — community engagement through citizen science.
  • Economic — lowers maintenance costs and reduces tree replacement expenses.
  • SDG alignment — contributes to SDGs 11, 13, and 15.

Who Benefits

  • Urban foresters — data-driven tree care.
  • Green-roof operators — precise irrigation.
  • Climate adaptation teams — resilience monitoring.
  • Community stewards — adopt-a-tree programs.
  • City planners — evidence-based green infrastructure.

Hardware Architecture

Key components

Every physical part that makes Eco Matrix resilient and accurate.

🧠
ESP32‑S3 Microcontroller

Dual‑core processor with 512 KB SRAM, 16 MB flash. Integrated Wi‑Fi/Bluetooth (optional).

🌱
Capacitive Soil Moisture

I²C interface with ±3% accuracy. Factory‑calibrated, supports per‑site calibration.

🌡️
DHT22 Temperature

Precision temperature probe, ±0.5°C accuracy, waterproof for outdoor use.

📡
LoRa SX1276 Transceiver

868/915 MHz, external dipole antenna, range up to 2 km (urban) / 5 km (rural).

🔋
Low Power

Very low Power consumption

🔌
Relay Output

24 V AC dry‑contact relay for solenoid valves, pumps, or other actuators.
